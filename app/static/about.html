About Beyond Identity

India Rights & Welfare Redressal

A secure national platform empowering transgender and gender-diverse citizens across India through verified affirmative listings, transparent discrimination incident redressal, statutory legal rights awareness, and clinical health guidance.

⚖️ Anchored in Supreme Court of India NALSA (2014) & Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019.
